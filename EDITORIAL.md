# Editorial guidelines

Not published, not linked, not rendered. Read this before drafting anything for `notes` or
`projects` — it exists to catch the tone before it ships, not to fix it after.

## Two readers, two registers

- **CV / Research / Publications** -- a committee member, collaborator, or seminar host.
  Expert, motivated, skimming fast. Write dense and precise. This reader wants information
  density, not warmth.
- **Notes / Projects** -- a sharp colleague one field over: a mechanics grad student, a
  faculty member in an adjacent structural area, or yourself in three years re-deriving
  something you've forgotten. Not a specialist who needs no explanation, not a lay reader
  who needs everything spelled out. Write like explaining at a whiteboard, not lecturing.

## Voice rules to check before drafting a note

- State what a result is verified against, every time.
- No unhedged claims outside work you've personally checked.
- No opinions about named people.
- Notes are "here's what I worked through," never "here's how this works" -- the first ages
  well, the second reads as performing authority you may not have yet.
