## Shake table outreach

As EERI UCSD Graduate Student Chapter president, I organized shake-table visits, resume
workshops, and office tours for the department's Master's students -- introducing them to the
San Diego structural engineering workforce before they're on the job market. Alongside that, I
mentored an undergraduate competition team through numerical modeling, construction, and
testing of a 6 ft balsa-wood building model for a miniature shake-table competition.
